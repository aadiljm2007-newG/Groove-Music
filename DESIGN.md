## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an uncompromising intersection of independent music zine culture and mid-century analog audio engineering. It channels the physical tactility of tactile hi-fi equipment—heavy toggle switches, rotary potentiometers, physical vinyl matrices, and mechanical transport controls—filtered through a raw, unapologetic Neo-Brutalist framework.

Targeted at audiophiles, vinyl collectors, underground tastemakers, and electronic music producers, the visual tone rejects sanitized SaaS patterns in favor of heavy industrial physicalism, deliberate asymmetry, and technical calibration aesthetics. Interfaces should feel tangible, mechanically sound, and stamped onto physical stock: durable, loud, and engineered for high-fidelity discovery.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-contrast physical tensions: tactile tactile substrates against pure light reflections, bound by heavy carbon lines and energized by high-visibility calibration dyes.

- **Primary (`#FFE600` / Electric Yellow):** Primary interactive focal points, cue points, active recording states, and high-priority action targets.
- **Secondary (`#A6FF00` / Cyber Lime):** Playback indicators, active pitch meters, frequency analyzers, and system telemetry markers.
- **Tertiary (`#FF5733` / Hyper Coral):** Hot cues, muting actions, physical cut-off points, and destructive/overdrive thresholds.
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base Substrate: Concrete Paper (`#ECEAE4`) for global canvas depths.
  - Surface Alt: Newsprint Cream (`#F4F0EA`) for secondary modular trays.
  - Interactive Surfaces: Pure Paper White (`#FFFFFF`) for crisp cards and transport containers.
  - Deep Wells: Structural Black (`#111111`) for persistent decks, vinyl wells, chassis dividers, and stroke boundaries.

## Typography

The typography couples raw editorial mass with technical playback readouts.

- **Primary Display & Headings (Space Grotesk):** Ultra-bold, geometric, slightly condensed rhythm with tight negative letter-spacing for artist titles, album names, and zine mastheads. Always uppercase for major headers (`display-xl`, `display-lg`).
- **Body & Editorial Copy (Space Grotesk):** Functional, robust grotesque shapes designed for high legibility over textured backgrounds. Set with confident body weights (`400`–`500`) to resist heavy visual surroundings.
- **Labels, Telemetry, and Metadynamics (Space Mono):** Fixed-width mechanical precision for track durations, BPM tallies, bitrates, audio matrix addresses, vinyl matrix etchings, and timestamp tickers. All labels and secondary metadata enforce monospace alignment to retain tabular instrumentation rigor.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an architectural industrial framework structured around visible physical enclosures:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views operate on a 12-column rigid modular grid with 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters and 2rem (`32px`) canvas offsets. Mobile views drop down to a dense 4-column layout with 1rem (`16px`) gutters and margins.
- **Modular Shells:** Content is grouped into discrete, structural containers reminiscent of modular rack-mount gear. Containers can span multi-column widths (e.g., 8-column primary turntable decks paired with 4-column master bus/tracklist monitors).
- **Rhythm & Gaps:** Component spacing strictly avoids fractional padding. Internal gaps scale through crisp multiples: `space-xs` (4px) for data tag grouping, `space-sm` (8px) for control clusters, `space-md` (16px) for module interiors, `space-lg` (24px) for component partitions, and `space-xl` (40px) between major architectural racks.

## Elevation & Depth

This system outright forbids gaussian blurs, soft drop-shadows, ambient feathering, or translucent glassmorphism. Depth is entirely mechanical, graphic, and mechanical:

- **Hard-Edged Offset Shadows:** Physical elevation is communicated exclusively through hard, unblurred drop-shadows cast in solid black (`#111111`) at a locked 45-degree trajectory:
  - Surface Elevation 1 (Interactive buttons, chips, stickers): `3px 3px 0px #111111`
  - Surface Elevation 2 (Cards, track bays, floating panels): `6px 6px 0px #111111`
  - Surface Elevation 3 (Major control consoles, modal overlays): `10px 10px 0px #111111`
- **Structural Lines:** Every card, container, button, and header frame is bound by solid 3px or 4px strokes in `#111111`.
- **Active State Depress:** Clicking or activating any elevated control physically shifts the element down and right to eliminate the offset drop-shadow (`transform: translate(3px, 3px); box-shadow: 0px 0px 0px #111111`), mimicking physical mechanical actuation.

## Shapes

The primary structural geometry is completely squared and mechanical (`0px` border radius). Cards, modal windows, hardware panels, input cells, and transport keys feature razor-sharp rectangular corners.

Intentional geometric exceptions are isolated to specific analog hardware paradigms:
- **Pill Badges:** Metadata tags, genre stickers, and vinyl speed indicators (`33 RPM` / `45 RPM`) use fully curved 9999px pills to mimic die-cut vinyl stickers.
- **Rotary Dials & Spindles:** Turntable platters, vinyl center labels, and potentiometer dials maintain strict 1:1 circular proportions with heavy borders.

## Components

### Buttons & Transport Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** High-contrast Electric Yellow (`#FFE600`) or Hyper Coral (`#FF5733`) background, 3px solid black border, `4px 4px 0px #111111` shadow, bold uppercase monospace or grotesque typography.
- **Transport Actuators (Play/Cue/Stop):** Chunky tactile buttons reminiscent of tape decks. Rectangular, padded (`16px 24px`), with bold vector icons and status labels. Active state eliminates offset shadow and displaces by 4px.
- **Ghost/Tertiary Actions:** White (`#FFFFFF`) or transparent fill, 3px black border, retaining the 3px offset drop shadow.

### Cards & Track Strips
- **Standard Card:** Crisp white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed in 3px solid black borders with `6px 6px 0px #111111` shadow. Padding locked to `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- **Tracklist Items:** Dense tabular strips arranged with concrete paper or white alternate backgrounds. Inactive items use a 2px bottom border. Hover states fill with Cyber Lime (`#A6FF00`) or Electric Yellow (`#FFE600`) with instant color transitions.

### Stickers & Status Pills
- **Die-cut Badges:** Monospace text set inside full pill containers or 0px angled labels, framed with 2px black borders and rotated slightly (-2° to +2°) to emulate zine sticker placement.
- **Live State Markers:** Cyber Lime (`#A6FF00`) or Hyper Coral (`#FF5733`) pills indicating status (e.g., `ON AIR`, `STEREO`, `LOSSLESS`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- **Input Fields:** Crisp white interiors, 3px black borders, recessed look created via a reversed inward offset border or zero shadow with 3px stroke. Typeface set in Space Mono for immediate visual distinction from editable data.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Solid square boxes with 3px borders. Checked state fills entirely with `#111111` containing an Electric Yellow (`#FFE600`) inset dot or heavy crosshair.

### Marquee Tickers
- **Now Playing / Telemetry Bar:** Continuous horizontal text ribbons bound by 3px top and bottom borders, filled with Electric Yellow or black, displaying uppercase metadata in Space Mono at infinite loop speeds.

### Vinyl Deck & Turntable Platter
- **Turntable Console:** Dark chassis panel (`#111111`) embedded in the primary layout, containing a concentric vinyl disk with grooved SVG ring lines, a rotating center label sticker with album art, a mechanical pitch fader with stepped tick marks, and an analog VU meter with redlining bars in Hyper Coral.
